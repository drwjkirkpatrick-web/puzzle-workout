## Hard rules

- https only. Hugging Face is blocked. No pages behind logins.
- Never copy from modern copyrighted books, test-prep companies, or any
  organization's current website.
- A book published in 1930 or earlier is public domain in the US (as of 2026).
  If you cannot verify a work's status, write an original puzzle instead.
- Every entry's `url` and `accessed` must match a page you fetched THIS session.
- Adapt: restate in your own modern English. Never paste long verbatim passages.
